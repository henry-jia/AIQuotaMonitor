# AIQuotaMonitor v1.8.3

发布日期 / Release date: 2026-11-13

## Codex Credit 日用量历史 / Codex Credit daily usage history

- **本地增量记录**：自动读取 ChatGPT Usage 页可见的 Credit 流水，按服务增量合并到 `%LOCALAPPDATA%\AIQuotaMonitor\credit-history.json`；已知记录不会因重启或重复抓取而重复计数。
- **记录起点与近 7 天摘要**：Credit 卡片显示已知记录的最早日期；悬停列出近 7 个自然日内有消耗的每日合计，零用量日期不显示。
- **独立按天图表**：点击 Credit 条打开每日柱状图，默认展示近 30 个自然日，可调整起止日期；同一天多笔流水自动合计。打开窗口时触发一次服务刷新并合并新记录。
- **固定绿色**：Credit 余额、余额条与每日柱形统一使用绿色 `#3FB950`，不再随普通配额状态色变化。
- **隐私与开关**：数据仅保存在本机，沿用现有「记录用量历史」开关；关闭后不再记录，已有数据保留。

ChatGPT Usage entries are incrementally merged into per-service local history at `%LOCALAPPDATA%\AIQuotaMonitor\credit-history.json`, without duplicating known entries after refreshes or restarts. The card shows the earliest known record date and a seven-day hover summary containing only days with usage. Clicking the Credits row opens a daily bar chart with a default 30-calendar-day range and adjustable start/end dates; opening it also refreshes the service and merges new entries. Multiple entries on the same day are summed. Credit balances and bars use a fixed green (`#3FB950`). History remains local and follows the existing Record usage history setting; turning it off stops new recording without deleting existing data.

## 升级 / Upgrade

1. 从托盘退出旧版本 / Exit the old app from the tray.
2. 替换 `AIQuotaMonitor.exe`，保留同目录的 `config.json` / Replace the executable and keep `config.json`.
3. 启动 1.8.3 并刷新 Codex 服务。Credit 流水会自动增量保存；点击 Credit 条查看图表 / Start 1.8.3 and refresh the Codex service. Credit entries are saved incrementally; click the Credits row to open the chart.
4. 只有网页当前可见且成功读取的流水会进入本地历史；更早日期的数据在首次读取后持续保留 / Only entries visible and successfully read on the page are imported; older fetched days remain available locally.
